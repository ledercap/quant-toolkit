# Sectors

Research built on the toolkit. Start with the [sector webpage design and build guide](DESIGN_GUIDE.md) for brand tokens, source standards, responsive layout and the website integration handoff.

## Current scope

Choose one sector, `robotics/` or `space/`, and begin with 5-10 **US public companies**. Separate direct exposure from adjacent businesses. Private and international companies may provide clearly labeled context, but are not the core coverage universe. Verify current listing status and keep the sector choice open until confirmed.

The analyst chooses the page format. First explain the business models, supply chain and the intersection of large revenue pools with fast growth. Then select 2-3 companies for deeper coverage. Refine data and calculations as analysis raises questions; a new data build is not a prerequisite.

## Research structure

One folder per sector; inside, one file per topic:

- `market-sizing.md`: market definitions, revenue pools, growth, periods and methods.
- `players.md`: the company universe, tickers, exchanges, exposure and inclusion reasons.
- `pick-and-shovel.md`: components, suppliers, customers and bottlenecks.
- `coverage/`: analyst coverage on selected companies, one file per company.
- `sources.md`: linked source register with document, observation and access dates.
- `web/`: optional webpage source, scoped CSS/assets and local preview/build instructions, following the [design guide](DESIGN_GUIDE.md#5-repository-and-integration-handoff).

Every material figure cites its source and date. Distinguish reported figures, calculations and estimates; explain missing data and unresolved discrepancies. Keep internal discussion, personal details and private source code out of public research.

The webpage is a source handoff for review and later website integration. Merging here does not deploy it or approve a production route.
